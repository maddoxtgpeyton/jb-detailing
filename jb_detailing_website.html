Customer Policy

Vehicle Detailing Service Terms & Paint Damage Policy

JB Detailing takes pride in providing professional, careful, and high-quality detailing services.

Paint & Existing Damage Disclaimer

JB Detailing is not responsible for pre-existing damage or damage that occurs due to the existing condition of a vehicle’s paint or exterior surfaces.

Before beginning service, customers should inform JB Detailing of any known issues, including:

  • Peeling or failing clear coat
  • Faded, oxidized, or deteriorated paint
  • Previous paintwork or body repairs
  • Chipped, cracked, bubbling, or flaking paint
  • Loose decals, wraps, badges, emblems, or trim
  • Scratches, dents, or other existing damage
  • Rust or corrosion
  • Previously repaired or damaged surfaces

Detailing may include cleaning, washing, drying, polishing, and other processes that can expose weaknesses in an already damaged or deteriorated surface. If paint, clear coat, trim, decals, badges, or other exterior materials are already compromised, JB Detailing is not responsible if they become loose, damaged, or separated during the detailing process.

Customer Responsibility

By booking a service, the customer agrees to disclose known damage or concerns before service begins. Customers are responsible for removing valuable, fragile, or personal belongings before the appointment.

JB Detailing is not responsible for personal belongings left inside the vehicle, pre-existing stains, odors, damage or defects, normal wear and tear, improperly installed aftermarket accessories, loose or deteriorated materials, or issues resulting from previous repairs, modifications, or aftermarket work.

Interior Detailing

JB Detailing will make reasonable efforts to clean and improve the condition of the vehicle’s interior. Some stains, odors, discoloration, pet hair, smoke smells, and other conditions may be permanent or require specialized treatment. Results vary depending on the age, condition, and type of material.

Exterior Detailing

Exterior services may include washing, drying, cleaning wheels and tires, applying protective products, and other procedures depending on the package selected. JB Detailing does not guarantee that every scratch, stain, swirl mark, oxidation spot, or paint imperfection can be removed.

Before & After Condition

For the protection of both the customer and JB Detailing, photographs may be taken before and after service to document the vehicle’s condition. These photos may be used to verify pre-existing damage and the condition of the vehicle at the time of service.

Weather & Service Conditions

Certain services may need to be rescheduled because of severe weather, extreme temperatures, unsafe working conditions, or other circumstances outside of JB Detailing’s control. JB Detailing reserves the right to refuse or stop a service if conditions are unsafe or if continuing could reasonably cause damage to the vehicle.

Customer Approval

By scheduling and/or authorizing a detailing service with JB Detailing, the customer acknowledges that they have read and understood these terms and agrees to the policies listed above.

JB Detailing will always make a reasonable effort to protect the vehicle and provide professional service. However, vehicles with existing damage, deteriorated materials, or previous repairs may carry additional risks during detailing.
